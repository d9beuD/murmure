## OpenAI Blossom

- Source: [OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/), downloaded on 20 August 2026.
- Files: `Sources/Entrevoix/Resources/ProviderLogos/openai-blossom-{black,white}.webp`.
- Processing: the official 716 × 716 PNG variants were scaled without alteration to 56 × 56 pixels and encoded as lossless WebP with transparency. They are displayed at 28 × 28 points for Retina density.
- Usage: shown only beside the OpenAI and OpenAI (Codex) providers; see the source guidelines for current trademark terms.
